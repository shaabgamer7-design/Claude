      Starting

    Works well

    A logo, channel name or date stamp that stays in one spot for the whole video, over a fairly plain area such as sky, a wall, a floor or a blurred background.

    Leaves a soft patch

    The box is filled by blending the pixels around its edges. Over faces, text or busy detail you will see a smooth smudge where the logo was. A tighter box gives a smaller patch.

    Won't work

    Watermarks that move around the frame, or one logo repeated across the whole picture. This tool only fills fixed boxes.

    Time and format

    The video plays through once while it is processed, so a 3-minute clip takes about 3 minutes. Keep the tab open. You get MP4 in Chrome, Edge and Safari, WebM in Firefox, with the sound kept.
