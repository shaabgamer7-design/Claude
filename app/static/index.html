Limits & performance

For the best results

  • Face video: 10–20 s, facing the camera, even light on the face, mouth mostly closed or softly talking, small natural head movement. Record in the format you post (vertical for Reels).
  • Photo avatar: sharp, front-facing, mouth closed, face at least 300 px tall. AI portraits work well.
  • Voice sample: 15–30 s of clean speech in a quiet room, no music.
  • Script: about 2.4 words per second, so 35 words for 15 s. Short sentences sync best.
