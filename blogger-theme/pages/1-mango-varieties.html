Chaunsa Mango

Chaunsa is the mango most Pakistanis wait for. It is very sweet, with smooth, fiberless flesh and a long season. That makes it the top choice for families, gifting and export.

  • TasteVery sweet, rich
  • TextureSmooth, fiberless
  • SeasonJuly to August
  • Best forEating fresh, gifting, export

White Chaunsa, Kala Chaunsa and other types of Chaunsa

White Chaunsa (Sufaid Chaunsa) is the early, pale-skinned type, prized for its aroma. Kala Chaunsa (Black Chaunsa) has a darker green skin and comes later in the season. Late Chaunsa stretches the harvest towards the end of summer. Ask us which type is being picked when you order.

Sindhri Mango

Sindhri is large, golden and fragrant, with sweet, smooth flesh. It is one of the first mangoes of the Pakistani season and one of the most popular for gifting.

  • TasteSweet, fragrant
  • TextureSmooth, fiberless
  • SeasonEarly season (from late May)
  • Best forEating fresh, wholesale, gifting

Anwar Ratol Mango

Anwar Ratol is small in size but big in aroma. Many people call it the sweetest mango in Pakistan. Its season is short, so it sells out fast.

  • TasteVery sweet, richly aromatic
  • TextureSoft, smooth
  • SizeSmall to medium
  • Best forEating fresh, gifting

Langra Mango

Langra keeps its green skin even when ripe. The flesh is juicy, with a sweet taste and a pleasant tangy edge. It is a traditional favourite across Punjab.

  • TasteSweet with a tangy edge
  • TextureSoft, juicy
  • SeasonMid season
  • Best forEating fresh, wholesale

Lator Mango

A later-season variety from our orchard, with firm, juicy flesh. It extends the season for buyers who want mangoes after the main harvest.

  • TasteSweet
  • TextureFirm, juicy
  • SeasonLate season
  • Best forEating fresh, wholesale

Other Popular Types of Mangoes in Pakistan

Beyond the varieties we grow, you will also hear about Fajri (a large, late-season mango), Saroli, Dusehri, Totapuri and Sanglakhi. Each region of Punjab and Sindh has its own favourites.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many types of mangoes are there in Pakistan?

Pakistan grows hundreds of mango varieties, but only about a dozen are grown commercially in large amounts. The best known are Chaunsa, Sindhri, Anwar Ratol, Langra, Fajri and Dusehri.

Which is the king of mangoes in Pakistan?

Chaunsa is usually called the king of Pakistani mangoes because it is so sweet and has a long season. Anwar Ratol lovers would disagree, though!

Sindhri vs Chaunsa: what is the difference?

Sindhri comes earlier in the season and is larger and more fragrant. Chaunsa comes later, is sweeter and richer, and lasts longer into the summer.

Which city is famous for mangoes in Pakistan?

Multan and the surrounding districts of southern Punjab, including Muzaffargarh where our farm is, are famous for Chaunsa, Anwar Ratol and Langra. In Sindh, Mirpur Khas is famous for Sindhri.

How many calories are in a Chaunsa mango?

Mango has roughly 60 calories per 100 grams of fruit. A medium Chaunsa has about 250–300 g of flesh, which comes to around 150–180 calories.
