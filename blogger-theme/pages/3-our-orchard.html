Ghulam Hussain Farms sits on the fertile plains near Head Panjnad, in Tehsil Alipur, District Muzaffargarh, in the heart of southern Punjab. Hot summers, rich river soil and generations of mango-growing heritage give our fruit its character.

Meet Ghulam Hussain

What began as a quiet dream in open soil has grown into a thriving orchard of sweetness and tradition.

Ghulam Hussain built the farm from scratch with grit and vision. He put years of hard work and deep respect for the land into planting the very first saplings. Today, every mango from our trees carries his promise: quality, careful hands-on care and the true taste of Punjab, sent straight from our orchard to your table.

Grown With Care

We keep our growing practices as natural as possible. We use chemical inputs carefully, only when the trees or the fruit need them, and we watch the fruit at every stage.

Careful cultivation

Our trees are tended by hand, using traditional knowledge passed down on our farm.

Responsible input use

Chemical inputs are used sparingly, only when the health of the trees or fruit requires it.

Picked at the right stage

Timing matters. We check maturity closely before any mango is harvested.

Focus on the fruit

Every decision on the farm has one goal: naturally good fruit, grown honestly.

From Tree to Box: The Journey of Every Mango

  1. Grow. Mango trees are tended through the year on our farm in Alipur.
  2. Care. Trees are looked after closely, with inputs used only when needed.
  3. Harvest. Mangoes are picked with attention to maturity and condition.
  4. Sort. Fruit is sorted by hand for size and quality.
  5. Pack. Mangoes are packed carefully to protect them on the road.
  6. Dispatch. Orders go out to families, wholesalers and export buyers.
