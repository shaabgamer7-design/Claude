What Affects the Price of Mangoes?

Variety

Anwar Ratol and White Chaunsa usually cost more than common varieties because their seasons are short.

Time in season

The first and last mangoes of a variety cost more. The middle of the season gives the best value.

Size & grade

Larger, export-grade fruit costs more than mixed sizes.

Delivery city

Delivery charges depend on how far your city is from Alipur.
