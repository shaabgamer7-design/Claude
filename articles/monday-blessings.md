# Monday Blessings: 120+ Messages, Prayers & Wishes for a Good Week

Monday blessings are short messages, prayers or good wishes you send at the start of the week to tell someone you're thinking of them. You can text one to a friend at 7 a.m., post it in the family group chat, or say it under your breath before you open your inbox. Below you'll find more than 120 of them, sorted by who you're sending them to.

Copy any line you like. Change a word or two so it sounds like you. That small edit is what makes a forwarded message feel personal instead of forwarded.

## How to Send a Monday Blessing That Actually Lands

People get a lot of forwarded "Good morning" images. They scroll past most of them. A few small habits make yours one of the ones they read.

**Add their name.** "Happy Monday, Sarah" gets a reply far more often than "Happy Monday." Two extra words.

**Mention one real thing.** "Good luck with the dentist today" or "Hope the new job's first week goes well" shows you were paying attention. A template can't do that.

**Pick the right time.** Early morning works for family and close friends. For coworkers, send it as the workday starts. For someone in another time zone, check their clock first. A blessing that pings at 3 a.m. isn't much of one.

**Match the person.** A scripture verse can mean a lot to a friend who shares your faith and feel awkward to one who doesn't. A meme is perfect for your college roommate and odd for your grandmother. Choose from the right section above.

**Don't send it to everyone.** Ten personal messages beat one broadcast to 300 contacts. People can tell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Monday blessing?

A Monday blessing is a short message, prayer or good wish shared at the start of the week. It's meant to encourage someone, ask for God's favor on their week, or simply let them know you're thinking of them. People send them by text, WhatsApp and social media, or say them as a personal prayer.

### What can I say to bless someone on Monday?

Keep it short and warm. "May this week be kind to you," "Praying for you as your week begins," or "Blessed Monday, may everything you start today go well" all work. Add the person's name and something specific about their week to make it personal.

### What is a good Monday prayer?

A simple one: "Lord, thank You for this new week. Guide my steps, bless my work and help me be kind to everyone I meet. Amen." Psalm 118:24 ("This is the day the Lord has made") is also a popular verse to pray or share on Monday mornings.

### How do you say Happy Monday in a nice way?

Try "Wishing you a great start to the week," "Hope your Monday treats you well," or "Blessed Monday to you." For a hard week, "Thinking of you this Monday" is softer than "Happy Monday."

### Is it okay to send Monday blessings to coworkers?

Yes. Keep them general and professional ("Wishing everyone a productive week") unless you know a colleague welcomes faith-based messages. In team channels, a short, upbeat note is usually best.
