# Monday Blessings: Keyword Deep Dive & Content Brief (wisharo.com)

## Keyword profile
- **Primary keyword:** monday blessings
- **Intent:** Informational, close to "copy-and-share" (people want ready-made text and images to send)
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate. Most of the top 10 results we looked at are thin pages that appear auto-generated, hosted on unrelated or hijacked subdomains (e.g. `forum.thebristolcable.org`, `istock.hardhattraining.com`, `*.arktimes.com`). Google may demote these at any time, so a well-organised page written by hand can realistically reach page 1.
- **Zero-click risk:** Medium. People need the full text to copy it, so an AI Overview or snippet can't satisfy the search completely. An AI Overview may still show 3–5 sample lines.
- **SERP features:** Image results are prominent (sunrise, coffee and floral "Monday blessings" images). Expect People Also Ask questions like "What is a Monday blessing?" and "What is a good Monday prayer?".

## What's ranking
- Formats: big listicles ("100+ Monday blessings quotes"), image galleries and template pages (CapCut).
- Common weakness: generic filler intros, no sorting by who the message is for, repeated text across many domains, no guidance on how to send.

## Gaps this article fills
1. Messages sorted by recipient (friends, family, partner, coworkers, a hard week)
2. A "hard week" section; competitors only offer cheerful lines
3. Workplace-appropriate wording, with guidance on when to leave out faith language
4. A "how to send one that lands" section: name, timing, personalisation
5. Bible verses cited with references (Ps 118:24, Lam 3:22–23, Prov 16:3)
